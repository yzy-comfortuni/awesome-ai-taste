# Awesome AI Taste

给 AI 查用的设计与写作规则。按任务选一类，先用 1–2 条规则，需要时再读正文，不加载全库。

改稿保留数字、限定、归因与完成状态，不补事实。文体与任务优先；来源的角色、权限和强制流程不自动继承。选读无需安装或运行代码。

## 学术论文

- [Ten Simple Rules for Typographically Appealing Scientific Texts](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008458) — 科研文本的字体、公式和表格排版指南，附正反例；主要面向美式英语，须服从目标期刊规范。[正文](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008458) <!-- article-copy --> · [Markdown 正文](vendor/articles/scientific-text-typography.md)<!-- /article-copy -->

  **规则：** 科研结果表的文字列左对齐，数值列右对齐并使用等宽数字，让不同位数的结果便于比较。[出处](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008458)

## 诗歌写作

- [More than the Birds, Bees, and Trees: A Closer Look at Writing Haibun](https://poets.org/text/more-birds-bees-and-trees-closer-look-writing-haibun) — Aimee Nezhukumatathil 的英语俳文写作讲义，说明散文与俳句如何相互照应；允许结构实验，不把五七五当跨语言通则。[正文](https://poets.org/text/more-birds-bees-and-trees-closer-look-writing-haibun) <!-- article-copy --> · [Markdown 摘要](vendor/articles/writing-haibun.md)<!-- /article-copy -->

  **规则：** 将散文与俳句合读，检查短诗的意象是否因前文交代的经历而获得更具体的意味。[出处](https://poets.org/text/more-birds-bees-and-trees-closer-look-writing-haibun)

## 小说写作

- [On the Many Different Engines That Power a Short Story](https://lithub.com/on-the-many-different-engines-that-power-a-short-story/) — Lincoln Michel 的英语短篇创作讲义，用词语联想推动故事，附试写与修改示例；不是通用情节公式。[正文](https://lithub.com/on-the-many-different-engines-that-power-a-short-story/) <!-- article-copy --> · [Markdown 摘要](vendor/articles/short-story-engines.md)<!-- /article-copy -->

  **规则：** 从首句圈出两三个关键词，列出 10–20 个联想词；试写时每句用到其中一个，修改时撤去限制，删改生硬重复。[出处](https://lithub.com/on-the-many-different-engines-that-power-a-short-story/)
